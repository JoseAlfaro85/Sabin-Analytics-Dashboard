Blog / Resource Digest

Content demand, risk, and recommended action - May 2026
  • Strategic finding: Sabin's strongest content demand is foundational vaccine education. The top three resource pages generated 1,053 sessions, led by Vaccines 101: Their History, How They Work, and Their Impact, which suggests audiences are using Sabin as an explainer source before they are looking for institutional news.
  • Strategic risk: some pages have enough demand to matter but are not holding attention. How Vaccines Protect You and Your Loved Ones had the lowest average engagement (16s), and the three priority pages combine 425 sessions with short engagement windows (16s, 20s, 21s), so the issue is likely page experience, intent match, or next-step clarity.
  • External context: Google's May core update and AI Search guidance make this more than an SEO housekeeping issue. Expert-led evergreen pages are where Sabin can show authority, but they need clear answers, internal pathways, fresh proof points, and useful media to keep visibility as search becomes more answer-oriented.
