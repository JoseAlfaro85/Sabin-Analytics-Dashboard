Signals to Watch

When a mention becomes meaningful
  • Escalate an outbreak, misinformation, or policy story when volume rises and credible local sources appear in the same window.
  • Separate owned Sabin activity, partner amplification, and earned conversation before describing reputation health.
  • Use curated examples to explain why a signal matters, not just that it appeared.
  • Turn repeated questions, criticism, or confusion into a recommendation for owned content or partner amplification.

Readout Questions

What the listening layer should answer
  • What is newly emerging that Sabin should know before it becomes a broader story?
  • Where is Sabin being mentioned by credible third parties, and does the mention support what we want to be known for?
  • Which vaccine confidence or outbreak-response narratives need a proactive message, partner share, or briefing?
  • Which topics are overperforming in listening but underrepresented in owned content?
